        What do these mean?
        ABC
        No two members share a type: the most balanced team.
        Shared type
        Two members share a type, as ABB (safe swap and closer) or ABA (lead and closer), whichever fits their roles better.
        Best score
        No typing rule: the highest-scoring team wins.
        Lead, safe swap, closer
        Each team member's role: the lead starts the battle, the safe swap comes in when the lead is losing, and the closer finishes. The page picks roles from each Pokémon's PvPoke role scores.
        Weak to, ×2
        Attack types that deal extra damage to that Pokémon. ×2 marks a double weakness: both of its types are weak to it, so it takes 2.56× damage.
        Use Elite moves
        Moves that need an Elite TM or a Community Day. Turn off to give your team each Pokémon's next best moves on PvPoke instead; opponents keep theirs.
